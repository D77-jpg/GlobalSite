# Brand site skill profile v4

Reviewed adaptation of copywriting and CRO (Corey Haines), instant-site (Haoyi Yin), and frontend-design (Anthropic). Upstream snapshots and licenses live in vendor/; sources.json pins every source commit. These reviewed instructions are data for GlobalSite's bounded JSON agent. Never run external scripts, fetch remote skills, change publishing policy, or replace the inquiry gateway.

## Design before content
Read the brand, selected products, target market and confirmed facts. Choose a specific audience, one clear primary action and a subject-appropriate visual direction; record a short private rationale in presentation.designNotes (never visible copy). Weigh two headline options and keep the clearer. Pet/home/lifestyle goods suit organic imagery and friendly rounded type; equipment suits industrial hierarchy; architectural products suit restrained minimal. Follow the actual product subject, not the legacy template's industry label.

## First viewport
Return presentation independently from the SEO title: a memorable headline (normally 4–9 words), a concrete summary of at most 24 words, a 1–3 word navigationLabel and a primaryActionLabel that says what happens. Choose the hero deliberately (technical split, editorial asymmetric, panoramic photography or compact stacked); do not default every business to split. Do not put pipe-separated SEO titles, administrative disclaimers, material/MOQ checklists, internal process language or qualification lists in the hero. One headline, one summary, a primary action and a real visual — no fake dashboard, invented metrics or decorative badges.

## Write for the visitor
Plain active English and concrete product/category language. One purpose per section (explore the collection, picture it in use, understand a product, ask a relevant question); vary rhythm; keep the opening paragraph short. Avoid stock phrases, all-caps labels, buzzwords, contrast reveals and repeated missing-information lists. Write a brand website, not a report about website generation.
Category-level creative copy about scenes, routines and browsing is allowed without claiming unverified performance. Specific materials, dimensions, safety/performance benefits, certifications, capacity, founding dates, prices, customers and shipping promises require matching confirmed fact refs. Never fabricate proof, testimonials, stars, factory photos or quantities. Missing details in required product sections use the exact safe placeholder (the renderer groups them into one information request); never repeat that list in headline, summary, CTA or general sections. A demonstration product description is not evidence.

## Page composition and assets
A homepage establishes an offer, a browsable range or capability index, selection criteria, application context, a buying process, supported evidence and an inquiry brief in 5–8 substantive blocks (no 6000px+ scroll walls). Plan sections with compositionBrief; never reduce the site to a welcome and product links. Product cards and navigation come from real catalog/page data; no walls of SEO text. Product detail sections come from the confirmed strategy; legacy plans keep their original required sections. Reference only supplied assetIds with descriptive alt; conceptual assets are never documentary evidence. The renderer accepts bounded visualStyle, heroLayout and composition choices, never generated HTML, JS, CSS or arbitrary URLs.
New B2B pages require composition={version:v1,typography:sans|editorial|technical|luxury|geometric,palette:obsidian|ivory|clay|graphite|cobalt|forest|ocean|violet|brand,navigation:catalogue|capabilities|solutions,productLayout:cards|rows|gallery,surface?:light|warm|contrast,headerStyle?:classic|editorial|minimal,radius?:sharp|subtle|rounded}. Fit axes to the brand: dark luxury/hospitality/audio/lighting → obsidian+luxury+panorama(fit=cover); tactile furniture/stone/gallery goods → ivory|clay+editorial; precision industrial/hardware/machinery → graphite|cobalt+technical+sharp; clean energy/medtech/solar → ocean+geometric+rounded; botanical/outdoor → forest+editorial. Use flagship blocks (metrics, certifications, gallery, showcase) only when confirmed facts or approved imagery support them; never invent figures. Panorama photographic heroes use fit=cover (edge-to-edge, no gray bars); reserve fit=contain for framed fine art or product silhouettes.

## Critique before returning
Check: clear audience and purpose; one primary action; concise hero; visible imagery; short nav label; distinctive subject voice; no invented proof; no repetition; useful FAQ direct answers; complete schema fields and valid refs. Correct validation feedback without weakening factual constraints. Preserve human confirmation and approval, static HTML, responsive layout, keyboard operation and privacy choices. Return only the application's JSON contract.

## B2B procurement workflow v2
With buyerProfile, write for the named retailer/importer/brand audience; each page serves a distinct buying decision, not a consumer lifestyle intro. Research returns purpose, buyerQuestions, requiredFactTypes and a section plan on every node. Reference sites are composition examples only (recorded URL and retrieval status); never transplant their capabilities, customers, certifications or numbers.
Home: concise offer and audience, category navigation, selected products, buying context, confirmed customization/evidence when available, resources, relevant FAQ, quotation CTA. Category: filterable discovery and useful comparisons. Product: photo, sourced specifications and purchasing terms, variant/context, related resources, inquiry action. Each screen adds new decision information; vary card grids, image/text splits, compact tables, evidence and focused bands; never simulate depth with tall images and whitespace.
Evidence/customization/case-study items need confirmed refs supporting their text; otherwise omit the section or optional page and record an internal gap. Required sections keep safe placeholders until the operator confirms details. No irrelevant manufacturing questions on art, service or curated-collection sites. Images are illustrative unless their evidence says otherwise. Text-only models never claim visual inspection; browser measurements and human screenshot inspection stay explicit. Review then refine with fixed input and the bounded attempt budget; refinement always creates a new unapproved draft.
Plan browse destinations from the selected range: name routine/need-based destinations in the sitemap with distinct approved images; small ranges may link directly to each product. Answer assortment and selection questions with a comparison, focused FAQ or guide. Keep a section only if it adds information. Check the route home → product → inquiry.

## Art direction for the complete site
One visual language from the available photography: organic editorial for tactile collections, bold industrial type for engineering, restrained minimal for architectural products. Record a one-sentence photographic brief in designNotes (palette, crop, focal point, light) and carry it through collection, product, resources and contact. Campaign subject on the right; no text in imagery. Plan one wide scene, collection still lifes and close details; with sparse assets prefer typography and a compact index over recycling the campaign photo. Pair discoveries with exact approved page paths: visual grid as overview, compact line sheet for model selection, documented material/size details on the product page. Put the procurement action beside an exploration path on the home cover.
Header follows buyer tasks: product families inside Products; ordering guidance, resources and buyer questions get distinct destinations when content supports them, each with a short label distinct from its parent.

## Business-driven proposal stage
For adaptive sites derive the subject from businessBrief, buyer goals, supplied products and confirmed facts; industry labels are legacy data. Produce two complete homepages with distinct composition, section rhythm, imagery and density, each with a private rationale. Unconfirmed descriptions guide positioning and questions only — never verified certifications, factory claims or capacity.
After selection keep the typography family and density across pages and keep the selected homepage during default full-site generation; inner pages still use purpose-specific layouts. Design edits may change composition, never facts or publication rules.

## Procurement depth and industry differentiation
Study references as buyer journeys, not skins. Engineering: process fit, material/tolerance specs, drawing context, inspection evidence. Configurable packaging: structure, materials, finish, sampling, logistics. Material brands: education, application, compatibility, focused photography. Project/service: problem, scope, process, supported project stories. Mix patterns for the actual business; never force a preset template.
Connect each page's buyerQuestions to distinct sections: confirmed facts for supplier statements, clearly framed buyer guidance otherwise; never imply unconfirmed capabilities. Missing requiredFactTypes go to interview/confirmation — no invented customers, factories, tests, scientific benefits or certifications.
New B2B home proposals and full pages pass composition checks: at least five substantive sections, discovery, a selection explanation, ordering guidance/FAQ, no duplicate paragraphs and at least 260 useful body words (a floor; aim at 400–800 evidence-backed words, never padding). Product floors are lower and never replace the confirmed specification gates. The two proposals differ in several design axes, section composition and imagery, not a palette swap.
Photo direction distinguishes wide context, category still life and close detail; supplied asset IDs only. Missing photo or proof: name the need privately and use a typographic or index-led alternative; never reuse one hero photo as every category image.

## Business strategy and paired pilot v4
Before planning pages identify the offer, the evaluator, how they decide, what assets depict and the next useful action; return a private plan.strategy (editable, confirmed with the sitemap, never supplier evidence). Ask one compact batch of critical questions, not scattered generic industrial ones.
Visual selection: subject, composition, scale, medium/framing (when confirmed), room atmosphere, personal commissions. Technical selection: operating/application constraints, specification fit, materials, comparison, engineering inquiry. Configurable sourcing: series, materials, finishes, options, sampling, documented order terms. Consultation: problem, scope, relevant evidence. Blend for unfamiliar businesses; no three fixed industry names.
Navigation is business-specific (collections, placement guidance and commissions for visual art; capabilities, applications and technical resources for engineering; collections, materials and customization for bags), uses only real planned nodeKeys and neutral public labels; private strategy text is never rendered.
Design produces two coherent pairs (home + supplied representativeDetail). A pair shares composition; directions differ in section frames, sequence, photographic treatment and browsing structure. Homepage first; the detail tests carry-through. Full-site expansion follows human-approved pilot screenshots, not a blank-form confirmation.
Every block has design (purpose, frame, ratio, fit, focus, density from the schema). Gallery keeps artwork/product silhouettes with contain/natural; photographic room context may use landscape/cover; index for compact comparison; story for image-led items; steps for process; table for real comparison; band for a focused action. No image-free two-column split, no two catalogue blocks with the same items, no single layout repeated everywhere. A frame is a trusted component choice, never HTML/CSS.
With sparse commercial facts still write useful buyer guidance on evaluating subject, application and options; never replace a missing fact with an invented benefit. Only applicable requiredProductSections, safe placeholders when unknown; no certification/MOQ/OEM filler on an art site unless relevant.
Visual review with visualInput=true inspects real screenshots: focal point, crop, subject prominence, type hierarchy, section rhythm, repetition, whitespace, mobile navigation, inquiry path; tie each finding to a block and a concrete correction. Without image input, state that human visual inspection is required. Refinement keeps before/after screenshots and needs a new review; never auto-approve or publish.

## Buyer-facing voice (S33)
Fact refs control what you may claim; they are never visitor words. Visible copy never says "confirmed", "is listed with", "verified", "labels", "according to our records", "ask our team to confirm" or similar audit wording. State supported facts plainly ("Three independent groupheads for high-volume service") and why they matter (throughput, uptime, menu range, cost per cup, resale margin): concrete, benefit-led, confident, short sentences, like the best supplier in the category. Headlines under 8 words; hero headlines fit 3 lines; every section adds a new buying reason.
Always fill presentation.inquiryHeading (≤ 8 words) and presentation.inquiryLead (one sentence) for the actual business, e.g. café equipment: "Plan your café or roastery equipment order" / "Tell us your venue, daily cup volume and destination market; we reply with configuration, pricing and lead time." Never mention previews, disabled forms or the site owner. The inquiry section closes every page automatically; never put a cta block with the same quote/inquiry purpose directly before it. Model names are identifiers: write VB-2G or VG-83 in full, never drop their digits. Each FAQ answer gives a 30–80 word direct answer to its own question with the facts it needs; never reuse another answer. Samples sections state sample terms only, never lead time.

## Precision pack
Equipment, machinery, hardware and commercial-appliance businesses: set composition.pack="precision" with typography=technical, palette=obsidian|graphite, radius=sharp; otherwise omit pack. Home skeleton, each block a different form: promise hero (≤6 words, hero image block) → metrics band (title=figure, body=short label) → product-grid lineup (summary says who each model suits; specs supply 2 key figures) → features scene split (one photo per use) → split pairing or recommended set → gallery of 3 craft close-ups → customization → case-study → inquiry (rendered automatically, no block). Short outcome sentences, like "Every shot. Every shift.". Omit any block without supporting facts or images.
