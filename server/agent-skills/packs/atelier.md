# Atelier rendering pack (composition.pack = "atelier")

Use `composition.pack = "atelier"` for design-led lifestyle, homeware, specialty consumer goods, tabletop, audio, lighting or tactile retail-wholesale brands, paired with `typography = "editorial"` or `"sans"`, `palette = "ivory"` or `"clay"`, and `radius = "rounded"`. Keep the same pack on every page of that direction.

## Home skeleton
Each block shifts pacing between photography and calm white space:
1. Editorial hero (≤7 words, natural light scene or clean studio product visual).
2. `category-grid` or `product-grid` showcase (clean studio cards with concise benefit summary and 2 confirmed highlights).
3. `features` ritual/use-case strip (compact photography cards, horizontally scrollable on 375px mobile).
4. `split` material & craft story (warm alternating surface).
5. `gallery` of tactile finish or detail close-ups (3 items).
6. `customization` or `process` wholesale/retail onboarding steps (confirmed options only).
7. `case-study` or `faq` (supported buyer questions, 30–80 word direct answers).
8. Inquiry section (rendered automatically; no duplicate closing CTA).

## Voice and discipline
Calm, concrete, design-literate copy. Short sentences focused on tactile materials, daily ritual, retail shelf appeal and wholesale readiness. Omit any section lacking confirmed facts or visuals.
